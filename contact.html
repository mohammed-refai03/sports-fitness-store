GOT QUESTIONS? WE'VE GOT ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common inquiries regarding international shipping, commercial equipment warranties, assembly guides, and order tracking.

Do you ship commercial gym equipment internationally?

Yes! We ship to over 100+ countries worldwide via trusted freight partners (FedEx Cargo, DHL Global Forwarding, and ocean freight for bulk gym setups). Customs duties, import taxes, and localized door-to-door delivery estimates are calculated automatically at checkout.

What warranty coverage comes with Stackly power racks & barbells?

All heavy-duty steel power racks, squat stands, and Olympic barbells come with a Limited Lifetime Structural Frame Warranty. Pulley cables, sealed bearings, and vinyl upholstery carry a 2-year full replacement warranty under normal commercial or residential use.

How do I track my freight order in real-time?

As soon as your shipment leaves our central distribution hub, you will receive an automated dispatch email with your freight tracking bill of lading (BOL) number. You can track real-time transit status on our site or directly via your assigned logistics carrier portal.

Do you offer custom laser logo branding & bulk facility pricing?

Absolutely. Commercial gyms, university athletic centers, and military facilities ordering bulk quantities qualify for tier-based volume discounts. We also provide custom laser-etched stainless steel branding plates and custom powder coat colors for power racks.

What is your 30-Day Hassle-Free Return Policy?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all apparel, accessories, and un-opened gear. If you are not 100% satisfied, simply initiate a return ticket through our portal. Freight return shipping fees apply for heavy strength machinery unless damaged upon initial transit arrival.

Can I book an on-site technician for equipment assembly?

Yes! White-glove assembly and professional technician dispatch are available in major metropolitan areas across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. Select "White Glove Installation" at checkout or contact our support team to schedule an appointment.
