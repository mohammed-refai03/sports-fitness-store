Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about freight shipping, power rack warranties, installation, and returns.

Do you offer free international shipping & freight delivery?

Yes! Free standard ground shipping is included on all orders over $150. Heavy freight shipments (power racks, functional trainers, treadmills) ship via insured freight carriers with real-time BOL tracking.

What warranty coverage comes with Stackly strength equipment?

All heavy-duty 11-gauge steel power racks, squat stands, and Olympic barbells come with a Limited Lifetime Structural Frame Warranty. Pulley cables, sealed bearings, and vinyl pads include 2 years of full replacement coverage.

Can I book professional white-glove equipment assembly?

Absolutely. White-glove assembly, room positioning, and packaging removal are available nationwide across North America, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. You can add assembly service directly during checkout or request technician dispatch.

What is your 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee policy?

We offer a hassle-free 30-day return window on all apparel, accessories, and un-opened gear. If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, initiate a return ticket through our support portal for a full refund.

Do you offer custom laser logo branding for commercial facilities?

Yes! Our in-house laser studio provides custom stainless steel logo plates, custom powder coat color finishes, and laser-etched uprights for commercial gyms, university weight rooms, and training centers.
